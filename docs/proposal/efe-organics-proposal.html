Where Efe stands today

Every Efe order currently reaches a customer through somebody else. That arrangement sells product, and it costs three things that do not appear on an invoice:

What is lost Why it matters
The margin The retail markup on every order stays with the reseller rather than with Efe.
The customer Efe does not know who bought, what they bought, or how to reach them again. There is no list to market to and nothing to build repeat business on.
Control Efe cannot run a promotion, change a price, launch a product or test an offer without asking someone else to do it.

What this delivers

Component What it does
Online shop The full storefront: catalogue, sizes, basket, checkout, mobile money and card. Efe's own channel, on Efe's own domain.
Back office Orders, stock with a full movement history, prices, promotions, customers, first-party analytics, and a record of every change and who made it.
History import Years of orders, customers and prices moved out of the current system in an afternoon, so nothing starts from zero.
WhatsApp agent Answers customer questions, checks stock, quotes delivery and takes orders on the channel where Ghanaian beauty sales actually close.
Content agents Drafts social posts, captions and product copy from your real ingredient lists. Nothing publishes without your approval.
Insight agent Ask the shop a question in plain English. What is selling, what to restock, which customers have stopped buying.

Everything the AI says comes from Efe's own data. Product facts come from your ingredient lists and price list, stock answers come from the live system, and nothing is published without a person approving it. That is the difference between a useful assistant and one that invents a claim about a cosmetic.
